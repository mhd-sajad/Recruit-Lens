Product: Recruit Lens, an explainable, evidence-grounded recruiter platform. Users: recruiters with invite-only accounts; each user sees only their own data.
Pipeline: JD and resumes in; parse and validate; chunk; hybrid retrieval of evidence per candidate (isolated per candidate); features; score with reasons; tier; agents write explanation, interview questions and email drafts; critique checks every generated claim against cited evidence; recruiter decides.
Agents (fixed-role, orchestrated, not autonomous): Retrieval, Scoring, Explainer, Interview Question, Email Draft, Critique (Self-RAG). Orchestrator: persisted state machine, resumable, per-candidate failure isolation, progress events.
Tiers (suggestions only): Strong Fit, Interview, Hold, Reject. A candidate missing a must-have skill cannot be Strong Fit. If resume parse confidence is low, the system abstains ("insufficient information") instead of scoring.
Rules: scoring never uses an LLM; no generated text without cited evidence; no email sent without an explicit human Send action; decisions append to an audit log; blind mode hides name, email and photo during review and before any LLM call.
Recruiter journey (17 screens): 1 login/logout; 2 dashboard (open jobs, stage counts, pending actions); 3 create job from pasted text or URL; 4 confirm extracted requirements (must-have vs nice-to-have, min years, education); 5 bulk resume upload with validation; 6 batch progress; 7 ranked table (sort, filter, tier, missing must-haves); 8 candidate detail (score, reasons, matched/inferred/missing skills, evidence highlights, timeline, what-if panel); 9 interview questions (edit/remove); 10 copilot (grounded Q&A with citations, says "not found" otherwise); 11 compare 2 to 3 candidates; 12 decision with note (shortlist, schedule interview, hold, reject, hire); 13 email draft, edit, approve, send; 14 post-interview scorecard and re-decision; 15 pipeline board; 16 reports (funnel, system-vs-HR agreement, metrics); 17 export (CSV/PDF), settings, blind-mode toggle.
Data entities: users, jobs, job_requirements, candidates, resumes (file metadata, extracted text, parse quality), chunks (with embeddings), scores, score_factors, artifacts (explanations, questions, drafts with critique status), decisions (append-only), interview_feedback, emails (draft, approved, sent, failed), audit_log, batch_runs, model_versions.
Non-goals: embedding fine-tuning, multi-company accounts, HR-platform integrations, calendar automation, video analysis, mobile app, OCR of scanned resumes.
